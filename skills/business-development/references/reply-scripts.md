# Reply scripts (answer from the outreach inbox, email_send op reply, account "outreach")

Principles: answer within hours, acknowledge, ask ONE question that moves it forward, never argue, never discount,
never invent. Propose specific times only after they show interest. Keep replies short (40-90 words), no header.

**Interested / "send the plan"**
"Thanks {first}, I'll send it by {day}. One question so it fits how you work: where do {the inputs} live today
({their likely tools})?" Then with the plan: "Here's the plan. The easiest next step is 20 minutes where I walk you
through it and you tell me what's wrong with it. {Day} {time} or {day} {time} ({their timezone})?"
Create the plan with document_create, from their audit (prospecting research) only. One page: the workflow today, what
the agent does, where their team approves, what it needs from them, how a pilot runs, what it would cost to run (only
if the user has given pricing; otherwise "pricing depends on scope, agreed after the call").

**"Send more info / a deck"**
"Sure. Rather than a generic deck I'll send a one-pager for {Company}: what it automates, where your team approves
and how a pilot runs. Which is the bigger headache right now, {A} or {B}? I'll build it around that one."

**"We already use {X}"**
"Makes sense, and I wouldn't replace it. The agent works on top of {X}: it handles the reading and re-typing before
anything reaches {X}. Is that step still manual for your team?"

**"No budget"**
"Understood. I can send the plan now so you have it when budget opens, or check back in {month}. Which is better?"
(No discounts. If they truly cannot afford it, close the lead politely.)

**"Not now" / "after {season}"**
"No problem. I'll send one short note after {season} and nothing before." Then lead_update stage nurture with next_at.

**Referral ("talk to {name}")**
"Thanks {first}, I'll reach out to {name} and mention you pointed me there." Only if they gave the name and it is
fine to mention them; save the new lead with the referral as the source.

**"Not interested" / "remove me"**
"Understood, I won't email again. Thanks for letting me know." (The pipeline already suppressed them.)

**"Did AI write this?"**
Always honest: "I use an agent I built to research and draft, and I read and approve every email myself. The point
about {Company} came from {source}." Never deny it.

**"How did you get my email?"**
"From {the page where it is published}. Happy to remove it, just say so."

**Pricing questions before a call**
"It depends on the workflow and volumes; a pilot is scoped to one workflow so it stays small. If you tell me {the
volume question}, I'll put a range in the plan." Only quote numbers the user has given.
