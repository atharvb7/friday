# Plays for our offers

Each play = who, the signal that shows the pain, the angle, and what to give first. Use only offers and proof that
exist in the business profile.

## Voice agents (inbound calls, booking, follow-up calls)

- **Who:** businesses that live on the phone: clinics, dentists, salons, real estate, restaurants, repair and home
  services, coaching institutes, logistics, clinics/labs, hotels.
- **Signals:** "call to book" as the only booking path; reviews that complain "nobody picks up" or "couldn't get
  through"; hiring receptionists/telecallers; limited opening hours; multiple branches on one number.
- **Angle:** missed calls are lost bookings. After-hours and peak-hour calls answered, booked and logged.
- **Give first:** a 60-90 second recorded demo of an agent answering a call *for their business* (their services,
  their hours), or "I called at 9pm and got voicemail. Here's what a caller would have heard instead."

## AI agents (lead qualification, support, internal ops)

- **Who:** SaaS and service companies with inbound leads or support volume; teams hiring SDRs, support reps, ops
  assistants.
- **Signals:** job posts for repetitive roles; a contact form with "we'll get back in 48h"; a chat widget that's
  only an FAQ; public docs and slow forum answers; growth after funding.
- **Angle:** response time and capacity without a hire. The agent qualifies/answers in minutes, the team gets clean
  handoffs.
- **Give first:** 3 specific ideas for where an agent removes work in *their* flow (from what you saw), or a tiny
  demo trained on their public docs.

## Automation (workflows, integrations, reporting)

- **Who:** agencies, e-commerce, ops-heavy SMBs, recruiters, finance/accounting firms.
- **Signals:** job posts mentioning spreadsheets, manual data entry, "coordinate between tools"; tool stack visible on
  their site (Shopify, HubSpot, Sheets, WhatsApp); slow order/lead follow-up.
- **Angle:** hours per week back, fewer errors, faster cycle; quote it as their time, not our tech.
- **Give first:** a short teardown: "here are the 3 handoffs I'd automate first and roughly what each saves."

## Freelance development (web apps, MVPs, integrations)

- **Who:** non-technical founders, startups with a small team, agencies needing overflow, businesses with an outdated
  site or app.
- **Signals:** posted jobs/briefs for developers; broken or slow site; "coming soon" for months; a new funding round
  with a tiny team; an agency hiring contract devs.
- **Angle:** shipped and maintainable, fast; show one relevant past build.
- **Give first:** a 5-point audit of their site/app (speed, mobile, broken flows) or a quick prototype screenshot.

## Our products

- Read each product's ICP and proof from the business profile. Pitch the product only where its signal is visible;
  never shoehorn. Founder-led tone: "I built X because..." works when true.

## Channels besides email

- **Warm first:** the user's existing network, past clients (ask for referrals and case-study permission), communities
  where the ICP hangs out. Draft these messages for the user to send personally.
- **Inbound loops:** each teardown or demo can become a public post (with permission or anonymised) that brings inbound.
- **LinkedIn:** draft connection notes and comments for the user to send manually.

## Prioritising

P0 = clear pain signal + reachable decision maker + offer with proof. Work P0 first, cap each batch at 15, and
review reply rates per segment weekly; double down on the segment that replies, drop the one that doesn't after
~40 sends.
