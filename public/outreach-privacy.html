Business outreach privacy notice

Last updated: 25 October 2026

If you received a business email from Atharv Bhosale, this page explains what information was used, where it came from and how to stop further emails.

Who is contacting you

Atharv Bhosale, an AI engineer based in Maharashtra, India, who builds AI agents and software for businesses. Contact: bhosaleatharv2@gmail.com.

What information is used

  • Business contact details: your name and role (when published), and the work or business email address.
  • Information about your company that is publicly available: its website, its public listing (for example on Google Maps), public reviews of the business, and public job posts or news.

Where it came from

Only public business sources: your company's own website, its public business listing and search results, and in some cases a business-email directory. The first email you received names the page where your address was found.

Why it is used

To send you a small number of one-to-one emails about services that seemed relevant to your role, based on what your business publishes. This relies on legitimate interest in business-to-business communication. No information is sold or shared for marketing. Emails are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools and approved by Atharv before they are sent.

Service providers

Email is sent through Google (Gmail). Drafting and classification use AI model providers (NVIDIA-hosted models and TypeSafe) that process the text of the email and the public information above for that purpose only.

How long it is kept

  • At most three emails are sent, and none after you reply "no".
  • If you object, your address goes on a do-not-contact list (kept only to make sure you are never emailed again) and the rest of your information is deleted.
  • Information about businesses that were never contacted is deleted within 6 months; other records within 12 months of the last contact.

Your choices

  • Reply "no" to any email, or write to the contact address above, and you will not be emailed again.
  • You can ask what information is held about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted.
